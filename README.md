# OBeautyCase

OBeautyCase is a professional OEM & ODM manufacturer specializing in cosmetic cases, makeup bags, nail cases, salon furniture, and beauty storage solutions.

Established in 2011, the company has over 26 years of manufacturing experience in the beauty and cosmetics industry. It operates under a unified brand system with **OBOX (domestic brand)** and **OBeautyCase (global export brand)**.

---

## About Us

We provide integrated manufacturing and export solutions for global beauty and lifestyle brands, including:

- Cosmetic Cases & Train Cases
- Makeup Bags & Beauty Organizers
- Nail Cases & Nail Tables
- Salon Furniture & Equipment
- Travel & Storage Solutions

We support OEM & ODM customization, private label development, and long-term B2B supply partnerships.
